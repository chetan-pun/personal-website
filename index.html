MY RESEARCH



1. GPT Semantic Cache: Reducing LLM Costs and Latency via Semantic Embedding Caching(preprint) View

This research introduces GPT Semantic Cache, a method designed to optimize the efficiency of applications powered by Large Language Models (LLMs). The primary goal is to reduce computational costs and latency by caching semantic embeddings of user queries, enabling faster and more cost-effective responses for repetitive and semantically similar queries.



It achieved up to 68.8% reduction in API calls across various query categories, significantly lowering operational costs. It delivered faster response times, improving user experience compared to traditional methods, and demonstrated exceptional reliability with positive hit rates exceeding 97%, ensuring high accuracy in retrieved cached responses.
